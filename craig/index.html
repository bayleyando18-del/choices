Competitors

Ten shops selling outdoor fabric, cushions or covers. Tap a name to open it. Under each: what its design does, and three things worth taking.

Cushion Factorycushionfactory.com.au

Open ↗

Busy but confident. Navy and white, big photos of finished cushions, a quote button on every screen.

  • Instant online quote for a custom size
  • Free fabric samples, offered early
  • Lifetime workmanship warranty, stated up front

Tropiquetropique.com.au

Open ↗

Bright and tropical. The product photos do the talking, and a price sits under every cushion.

  • Sunbrella range gets its own collection page
  • "From $" pricing with the sale price beside it
  • Click and collect for local customers

Outdoor Interiorsoutdoorinteriors.com.au

Open ↗

Resort colours on white, with trust lines everywhere: free shipping, a five-year fade warranty, Afterpay.

  • Free shipping banner at the top of every page
  • Reviews that say "still good after six years"
  • Quick view on each product tile

Cushion Housecushionhouseaustralia.com.au

Open ↗

Plain, factory-direct feel. A red mark, and indoor and outdoor split cleanly at the top.

  • Size, foam and fabric builder that prices as you go
  • Express ten-day dispatch promise
  • Free samples and a lifetime guarantee

Perth Outdoor Cushionsperthoutdoorcushions.com.au

Open ↗

A small Shopify store, charcoal on white. Six furniture types on the home page and little else.

  • Shop by furniture type, chair to sun lounge
  • "From $" starting price on every type
  • Sixteen named reviews with dates

The Fabric Editorsthefabriceditors.com.au

Open ↗

Black and white, gallery feel. The fabric is the picture; the designer logos give it weight.

  • Sample request on every fabric
  • 15% off orders over ten metres
  • Brand logos as a trust row

Warwick Fabricswarwick.com.au

Open ↗

Big, calm and white. Photos of rooms, not rolls, with one burnt-orange accent.

  • Filter by colour palette, thirteen named colours
  • SunDec outdoor range has its own page
  • A tool to see the fabric on a piece of furniture

Basil Bangsbasilbangs.com

Open ↗

Near-black type, lifestyle photos, stripes everywhere. Feels like a fashion label, not a shed.

  • Shop by colour family
  • Press logos as a trust row
  • 10% off for joining the list

Sunbrellasunbrella.com

Open ↗

The maker's own site. White space, big fabric close-ups, plain sans-serif type.

  • Browse fabrics by use: "for outdoor cushions"
  • Colourway swatches on every product
  • Find-a-retailer, which could list Craig

Maharammaharam.com

Open ↗

Museum quiet. Black, white and grey, a strict photo grid, and almost no words.

  • Browse by palette
  • A compare tool for fabrics
  • Stories as content, not blog posts
